Questions

Why can't I just fix it myself?

You can — once you know what's broken and what “fixed” looks like. Most owners don't know their address is wrong in an AI answer, and terms like structured data aren't part of running a business. We diagnose, fix, and prove the change, while you keep running your shop.

Do you need my passwords?

Never. Every platform has an invite or manager function. You grant access, you keep ownership, and you can revoke us anytime.

How long until AI answers update?

Sources update at different speeds — some within days, most within weeks. That's why every paid engagement includes a re-test 30–60 days later, with screenshots.

What does the free check include?

A 2-page mini report: how three AI assistants currently answer real customer questions about your business, the three most damaging issues, and real screenshots. No signup, no obligation.

Do you only work with F&B?

We started in F&B because that's the industry we come from. The method works for any local business — clinics, gyms, salons, studios.

Who is behind showsup?

A Singapore F&B operations professional — years spent running real kitchens and outlets, now fixing how AI describes them. We keep a low profile and let the work speak: your free check comes with real screenshots you can verify yourself before paying anything.

My website is on Shopify / Wix. Is this still useful for me?

Yes — keep your site, we don't touch it. AI assistants mostly read things outside your website: your Google Business Profile, maps, review sites and platform listings. That's the layer we check, fix and maintain. We only build websites for businesses that have none, or one that's badly outdated.

How do I pay?

PayNow or bank transfer, against an itemised invoice. For the audit, you pay only after the report is in your hands.

What if I'm not happy with the work?

Every fix we make is re-tested 30–60 days later with screenshots. If a fact we corrected goes wrong again within that window, we work on it again at no charge. Websites include two revision rounds.
